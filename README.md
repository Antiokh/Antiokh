Belgrade, Serbia

I help businesses make technology economically useful: increase revenue, reduce operating cost, remove critical dependency on individual employees and vendors, and give management clear control over systems, workload, spend, and risk.

I have 18+ years of experience across founder-led businesses, banking, enterprise IT, and public-sector systems. I work best when the business is already real, but technology has become fragmented, opaque, expensive, or too dependent on informal knowledge.

## Selected business results

- Helped a service business achieve approximately **10× revenue growth in about three months** after redesigning customer intake, operations, and product presentation; growth was organic, without paid advertising.
- Reduced routine client-service work from **hours to minutes** through internal systems, integrations, and automation.
- As part of the management team at ZIL, contributed to the anti-crisis turnaround that brought the organisation out of approximately **RUB 30m in accumulated debt**; within IT, focused spending on operational value and replaced unmanaged support with measurable service operations.
- Moved **100+ employees** to secure remote work during COVID without interrupting operations.
- Accelerated heavy reporting exports by approximately **20×** in a city-scale operational system serving **100+ institutions**.
- Built an IT operating model at ZIL that remained stable after my departure and through subsequent leadership changes.

## What I usually fix

- IT spending is difficult to connect to business value.
- Delivery slows while responsibilities remain unclear across employees, teams, and vendors.
- Critical processes depend on spreadsheets, chats, manual handoffs, or individual memory.
- Systems are fragmented and nobody owns what happens between them.
- A key employee, contractor, or platform has become an operational dependency.
- Management cannot see workload, bottlenecks, service quality, system risk, or the real cost of change.
- Technology exists, but adoption, ownership, and operating discipline lag behind implementation.

## How I work

I treat architecture as a management instrument, not a diagram. Critical systems, processes, integrations, vendors, and risks need explicit ownership. Work needs to become observable. Technology spending needs to map to capability and value. Automation should remove fragile handoffs, not merely add more software.

I am hands-on when that reduces uncertainty or accelerates delivery, but the objective is a system the organisation can operate without depending on me.
